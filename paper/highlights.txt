Highlights

- Pre-registered, frozen-before-testing evaluation of twelve pneumothorax models (54 runs).
- Gradient-reversal debiasing with pseudo device labels enlarged the device shortcut.
- Every model, including a radiology foundation model, detected treated cases more easily.
- In eight of twelve models a device alone raised the pneumothorax score in negatives.
- The adversary reached chance in training, yet device information remained recoverable.
